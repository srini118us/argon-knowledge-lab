# Cafeteria services agreement: summary

*Facilities summary for staff. Questions go to the Facilities team, not to the catering staff on site.*

## Who runs the cafeteria

Our cafeteria is operated by **Brightplate Catering** under a services agreement that took effect at the start of the year. Brightplate prepares and serves food on site, keeps the kitchen and serving areas clean, and stocks the self service fridges and coffee station.

The agreement replaced the previous arrangement after Facilities ran a selection process last year. Staff feedback from the office survey was a big part of that decision: people asked for more variety, better options for dietary needs and a clearer way to raise complaints. The new agreement addresses all three.

## Service hours

The cafeteria is staffed **07:00 to 15:00 on weekdays**.

- Breakfast is served from opening.
- Lunch service runs through the middle of the day; the daily menu is posted on the screen by the entrance and on the intranet.
- The coffee station and fridges remain available outside staffed hours, but hot food is only served while Brightplate staff are on site.

There is no staffed service on weekends or public holidays. If you are working late or on a weekend for an incident or a release, plan accordingly; the vending area is still open.

## What is included

Under the agreement Brightplate provides:

- a daily hot meal selection, with at least one vegetarian option every day
- a salad and sandwich counter
- clearly labelled allergen information for every item
- catering for internal meetings and events, ordered in advance through Facilities
- cleaning of the cafeteria area during and after service

Special dietary requests (allergies, religious requirements and so on) can be arranged. Speak to the Brightplate manager on site or contact Facilities, and they will work out what can be provided.

## Meeting and event catering

If you are hosting a workshop, an offsite day held in the office or a team celebration, order catering through the Facilities request form rather than asking the kitchen directly. Facilities consolidates orders so that Brightplate can plan staffing and stock. Give as much notice as you can, particularly for larger groups. Late requests are handled on a best effort basis.

## Feedback and complaints

The agreement includes a feedback process, which was one of the main things staff asked for:

1. For small issues on the day (a cold dish, an empty fridge), tell the Brightplate staff on site. They would rather fix it immediately.
2. For anything else, use the cafeteria feedback form on the intranet. Facilities reviews submissions and raises recurring issues at its regular reviews with Brightplate.
3. Anything involving food safety or an allergic reaction should be reported to Facilities straight away and, where needed, to first aid.

## Things to remember

- Please return trays and crockery to the collection point.
- Label anything you put in the shared fridges. Unlabelled items are cleared out at the end of each week.
- The cafeteria is a shared space. If you need to take a call, step outside.
