# Reminder: benefits open enrollment is open

Hi everyone,

This is a friendly reminder from HR that benefits open enrollment is underway. **Open enrollment closes on 2025-11-15.** After that date, you will not be able to change your benefit elections until the next open enrollment period, unless you have a qualifying life event.

We know this lands in the same season as planning cycles, peak shipping prep and everything else, so we are keeping this short. Please set aside some time before the deadline to review your choices, even if you plan to keep everything the same.

## Why it is worth a look this year

Every year a few people discover in the middle of the following year that their elections no longer match their situation. Maybe a family member joined their household, maybe their health needs changed, maybe they meant to start contributing to a spending account and never got round to it. Open enrollment is the easiest time to fix all of that.

Some things to think about:

- **Has your household changed?** Marriage, a new partner, a new child, or a dependent ageing off your plan all affect who should be covered.
- **Do your current plans still fit?** Look back at how you actually used your coverage this year. If you barely used it, or used it a lot, a different plan may make more sense.
- **Are your beneficiaries up to date?** People forget this one. It takes two minutes and matters a great deal.
- **Spending and savings accounts.** If you use them, your elections usually need to be renewed each year. Do not assume they roll over.

## If you are new this year

If you joined Northwind Logistics recently and already made elections as a new hire, you still need to take part in open enrollment. New hire elections cover you until the next plan year starts; open enrollment is where you choose what applies after that. It is also a good chance to correct anything you picked in a hurry during your first week.

## How to enroll

1. Sign in to the benefits portal through the employee intranet.
2. Review your current elections on the summary page.
3. Make any changes, or confirm that you want to keep your current elections.
4. Review the confirmation page carefully before you submit.
5. Save or print your confirmation for your records.

If you do nothing, some elections may carry over and some may not. The safest approach is to log in and actively confirm everything.

## Need help?

- **Benefits information sessions.** HR is running drop-in sessions during the enrollment window. Check the intranet events calendar for times. Sessions are recorded for anyone who cannot attend live, including colleagues working warehouse shifts.
- **Questions about your specific situation.** Contact HR through the HR help desk on the intranet. Please do not post personal details in team chat channels.
- **Plan documents.** Full plan descriptions are available in the benefits portal. They are long, but the summary pages at the front of each one cover the main points.

## A note for managers

Please give your teams a little time to complete enrollment, especially those on shift schedules who may not sit at a computer during the day. A quick mention in your next team meeting helps more than you might think.

## Key date

| What | When |
|---|---|
| Open enrollment closes | 2025-11-15 |

That is the one date to remember. Please do not leave it until the last day; the portal tends to be busy, and the help desk queue gets long.

Thank you, and take care of yourselves.
