## 1. Purpose

Customers and regulators require that some personal data stays within defined regions. This standard sets where vendors may process Northwind personal data. A vendor's processing region is the hosting region stated in its contract.

## 2. Rules

**DR-1** C2 or higher data about EU data subjects may be processed and stored only by vendors whose contracted hosting region is the EU.

**DR-2** C2 or higher data about US or APAC data subjects may be processed in any contracted region.

**DR-3** C1 data has no residency restriction.

## 3. Waivers

Residency rules are legal obligations and cannot be waived through the exception register.
