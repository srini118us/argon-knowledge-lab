Waivers apply only as described in ARB-CHARTER CH-2.3. Expired waivers are kept in the register for audit purposes.

**W-07** Waives SEC-POL SP-3.1 for vendor Parcelytics: standard HTTPS is accepted in place of mTLS while Parcelytics completes its mTLS migration. Scope: only integrations whose data subjects are all in the US. Valid from 2026-03-01 to 2026-12-31.

**W-12** Waives SEC-POL SP-4.1 for vendor InsightHub: retention of up to 60 days is accepted while InsightHub completes its automated deletion service. Scope: retention of no more than 60 days. Valid from 2026-01-15 to 2026-11-30.

**W-15** Waives SEC-POL SP-4.2 for vendor RouteMind: data may be stored without encryption at rest until the vendor delivers encryption. Compensating control, carried as part of the resulting condition: Northwind replaces customer and driver names and IDs with pseudonyms before sending. Valid from 2026-06-01 to 2027-03-31.

**W-03** Waived NET-STD NZ-2 for the legacy fleet control service. Valid from 2025-09-01 to 2026-02-28. Retired; kept for audit.
