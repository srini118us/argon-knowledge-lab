## Freeze windows

| Window | Start | End | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| Peak season freeze | 2026-12-14 | 2027-01-04 | Holiday shipping peak |

Both dates are inclusive.

**CF-1** A production go live inside a freeze window is approved only on condition that the go live moves outside the window. The ARB records this as a condition, not a violation.

**CF-2** Internal configuration changes with no customer impact are not affected by the freeze.
