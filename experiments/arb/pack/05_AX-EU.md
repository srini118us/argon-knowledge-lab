## 1. Scope

This annex applies to personal data about data subjects in the European Union, wherever Northwind or its vendors process it. Personal data means C2 or higher data under DCS-01. It adds to, and where stricter replaces, the global security policy for that data.

## 2. Retention

**AX-EU-1** A vendor may retain C2 or higher data about EU data subjects for at most 14 days after receipt.

## 3. Relationship to waivers

**AX-EU-2** Waivers granted against the global security policy do not waive this annex. An exception to this annex requires the written approval of the Data Protection Officer, Europe, and no such exception is currently in force.
